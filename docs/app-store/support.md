# Pedos Support

Pedos records your farts, finds the note of each one, and plays songs with them, using only your
recordings as sound.

**Contact:** <support email>. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the Pedos version
(Profile tab, under About).

## How it works

1. **Record.** On the Record tab, tap the big red button, fart, and tap Stop. One fart per
   recording.
2. **Save.** Pedos finds the fart's note, like G3, or tells you it's noise-like.
3. **Collect notes.** Every song needs a different fart for each of its notes. The Compose tab
   shows how close each song is and which notes it still needs.
4. **Make a Toot.** Open an unlocked song to hear it played by your farts, then save it to Toots.

## Questions

**Pedos can't use the microphone.**
Open Settings › Pedos and turn on Microphone. Pedos only listens while you're recording.

**It says "No steady note this time".**
Some farts are just noise, with no clear pitch. The recording is still saved, but it can't play
melody notes. Try again: longer, steadier farts are more likely to hold a note.

**It says "Didn't catch that".**
The take was empty: Stop was tapped right away. Tap Record, then Stop after the fart.

**It says "We heard more than one".**
Pedos keeps the first fart in a recording. Record one fart at a time.

**Why is a song still locked?**
Each note in a song needs its own fart close to that note. Open the locked song in Compose to see
which notes are missing, then record more.

**Where are my recordings? Can you recover them?**
They stay on your iPhone, inside the app, and are part of your iPhone's own backup if you have
backups turned on. We never receive them, so we can't recover them. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Where do the songs come from?**
Every song comes from a public-domain score in the PDMX dataset.
