# Pedos Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

Pedos is an iPhone app that records your farts, finds the note of each one, and plays songs with
them. This policy explains what happens to your data. The short version: **it stays on your
iPhone.**

## What Pedos keeps, and where

- **Your recordings.** When you tap Record, Pedos uses your iPhone's microphone until you tap Stop.
  If you save the take, it's kept as an audio file inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. If
  you discard it, it's deleted.
- **What Pedos works out from them.** Pedos analyzes each saved recording on your iPhone to find
  its note, and keeps that result next to the recording.
- **The songs you make (Toots).** They're made on your iPhone from your recordings.
- **One setting:** whether you've already seen the welcome screen.

Pedos only uses the microphone while you're recording, and only after you've allowed it. You can
turn microphone access off at any time in Settings › Pedos.

## What Pedos doesn't do

- It doesn't send your recordings, or anything else, to us or to anyone. The app makes no network
  connections.
- It has no accounts or sign-in.
- It has no ads, analytics or tracking, and contains no third-party code that does.
- It doesn't sell or share data, because it doesn't collect any.

## Backups

Your recordings are part of your iPhone's own backup (iCloud or your computer) if you have backups
turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple under your account; we can't access them.

## Deleting your data

Delete a recording or a Toot in the app, or delete the Pedos app to remove everything it stored on
your iPhone.

## Children

Pedos doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Pedos ever starts
sending data off your iPhone, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will say so
first.
