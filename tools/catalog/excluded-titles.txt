#     or a lyricist whose words were written with the music (as titles-02.txt counts them). PDMX takes a
#     score's license from its uploader, and an uploader can't put someone else's song in the public
#     domain (ADR 0003, #312).
#   - not one tune: a collection, an opera or suite, a title many pieces share, or one that names no
#     piece we can tell.
#   - the same tune as a Song in the catalog or a better-ranked title, under another name.
#   - the importer's pick isn't the tune most of its scores carry, as titles-02.txt found.
#   - an offensive title.
