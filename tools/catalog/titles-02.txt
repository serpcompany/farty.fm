# The second PDMX batch (#202): more well-known tunes that are public domain as compositions,
# none already in catalog/catalog.json or titles-01.txt.
# id | catalog title | other titles a score of the same Song may carry (optional, ; separated)
# A score matches when its title, its title before " - ", "(", "[" or ":", or its MuseScore song
# name is one of these.
# Left out: titles no pickable PDMX score matches, and titles whose matching scores include a
# different tune under the same name (a hymn set to several tunes, or a copyrighted song).
# Every tune here is public domain in the US and where copyright lasts the author's life plus 70
# years. The authors are the composer and, for a song whose words were written with the music, the
# lyricist (as the EU counts it); words added later, as on "The Teddy Bears' Picnic" or "Hava
# Nagila", don't count. So "Bye Bye Blackbird", "Take Me Out to the Ball Game", "O Sole Mio",
# "Cielito Lindo", "When Irish Eyes Are Smiling" and "O mio babbino caro" wait for a later batch.
# Also left out: titles whose pick, as the importer reads it, isn't the tune most scores of that
# title carry: the highest track is an accompaniment or a one-note ostinato ("Go Tell It on the
# Mountain", "Molly Malone", "The Ash Grove"), or the score sets another tune of the same name
# ("Here We Come A-Wassailing").
