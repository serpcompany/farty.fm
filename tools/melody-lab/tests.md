# The tests

What each of the reference engine's unit tests (`test_*.py`) checks, and why its cases are what they are. `uv run pytest ..` from `tools/melody-lab` runs them with the catalog importer's ([README](README.md)).

- **The rest:** Recording Analysis on synthetic tones and two fixtures, the costs, Melody Fit, `plan()` and `render()` on synthetic Recordings, the importer on generated MIDI, where a score's bars start (a pickup, a bar split at a repeat, meter changes, 6/8, and an excerpt's own beats), `evaluate.py`, the golden writer, and the golden data itself (every Arrangement, Song and fit). `test_export_golden.py` also checks that the export refuses a Song whose notes don't start in strictly increasing order (#287).
- **`test_needed_seconds.py`** checks that the length a locked Song says it needs (`needed_seconds()`) unlocks it, for a fart 3 semitones or an octave from each missing note.
- **`test_placed.py`** checks a Recording placed on a note by ear (`placed()`, ADR 0015): it counts as Melodic at that note, left out it changes nothing, and a placement outlasts a Recording Pitch a later analysis finds (ADR 0017). On the small library, Recording `065` placed on C2 unlocks two locked Songs, Jingle Bells and Row, Row, Row Your Boat, the same Arrangements the web's Auto-map tests (`apps/web/src/lib/engine/`) check. It uses the small library because the full one unlocks every golden Song, and no Noise-like fixture placed on C2 to B4 unlocks one more Song without `002`, its longest fart. `065` is Noise-like and 1.49 s long; `009` was Noise-like too until the second pitch window (#282) found it near G1.
- **`test_trim.py`** checks a Trim (ADR 0019): it is the fart between the handles and always lies within them, Songs play only that part and Melody Fit counts its length, its pitch isn't read again, and its lead-in never reaches before the start handle; on synthetic bursts, the golden data and Recording `065` (`apps/web/src/lib/analysis/trim.test.ts` checks the same bounds). `065` opens with a 30 ms blip at 0.59 s, 0.3 s before its two loud bursts; analysis keeps the blip, so a Song starts the note on it, and handles at 0.8 and 2.3 s keep the bursts. A Trim to one burst (0.25 s) is too short for the note it played. Mary Had a Little Lamb plays `002` (4.04 s, B♭2) on its 1.4 s notes: trimmed to 1.5 s it still covers them, and to 0.5 s it doesn't.
- **`test_lead_in.py`** checks a fart's lead-in (#287): the sound joined to the attack, never room noise before a longer gap and at most 0.25 s; it plays before the beat in the rest before its note, never in the gap after the note before nor before the Song's start, the attack lands on the beat and the level is still set from it, notes written out of start order place the same, and a fart played past its written end holds back the next lead-in. Read from the start of the lead-in, its quiet 0.3 s would set the note 28 dB louder; only the 3 ms fade-in, now on the lead-in instead of the attack, moves the level, by 0.1 dB. `009`'s attack, 0.217 s into the take, is a low rumble, but it opens on a quieter G3 from about 0.03 s. With `002`, `032` and `022` gone, `009` placed on G3 plays When the Saints' first note, on the half beat (0.2 s), so 0.2 s of its opening plays before it, and the Song still passes the bar. `apps/web/src/lib/engine/renderer.test.ts` checks the same on the web, given the notes in the same order, since the port returns its events in the order given.
- **`test_reattack.py`** checks the re-attack bar (`added_reattacks()`, ADR 0016): a re-triggered fart is caught whether it has one burst or two, as farts like `002`, `084` or `032` have their own second burst, and a fart's own second burst alone isn't.
- **`test_short_notes.py`** checks that a note shorter than the gap before the next has no time left to sound, not a negative one (#332). Its two-ornament melody is the smallest that crashed before #332, as Bolero did; `apps/web/src/lib/engine/auto-map.test.ts` checks the same on the web.
- **`test_estimate_pitch.py`** checks the Recording Pitch's second window (#282): it counts its coverage as the head does, so a few quiet frames at the end of a short first burst never make a note (#295). The owner's fart `107` has a 0.257 s first burst (45 frames), voiced only in its last 4, at pYIN's 45 Hz floor. Its other case is shaped like `009`: a long first burst whose tone starts late.
