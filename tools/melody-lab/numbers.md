# Why the numbers are what they are

The reference engine's thresholds and costs, how each was chosen, and what the web's port matches. The engine's files and commands are in the [README](README.md), and what the tests check in [The tests](tests.md).

- **The fart** (`note_segment()`) starts 8 ms before the level first comes within 12 dB of its peak, so a slow swell doesn't make the note sound late, and ends where the sound fades out, after the last 50 ms level within 30 dB of its peak. Songs play it from start to end, every burst included (ADR 0016). Its first burst, which Recording Analysis reads the pitch from so a second burst at another pitch doesn't blur it, ends at the first clear re-attack: on a level smoothed over 50 ms, which removes the "brrrr" flutter above about 15 Hz, a dip of 6 dB below the running maximum and then a rise of 4 dB, after the first 60 ms. Until ADR 0016 Songs stopped there too. `evaluate.py` finds re-attacks the same way, starting again from each one's own peak, and counts a note when its render holds one the dry stretch of its fart doesn't have within 30 ms (`REATTACK_MATCH_S`), so a fart re-triggered after its own second burst still counts.
- **The lead-in** (`lead_in_start()`, #287) is the sound joined to the attack: from the attack frame it walks back over every 5 ms level within 30 dB of the peak, as the fart's end does, and over quieter gaps of up to 50 ms, and starts 8 ms before the earliest such level. A sound before a longer gap isn't the fart's: on the owner's room-noisy phone takes (105 to 110, #295) it's room noise, up to 2 s of it. It is at most 0.25 s, for a room loud enough to join its noise to the attack: `009`'s 0.215 s G3 opening, and no more room noise. `render()` lands the attack on the note's beat and plays the lead-in just before it, as far back as it reaches but never before the notes before it end as written or stop playing, nor before the Song's start, so it never fills the gap between two notes or mixes over another fart: a lead-in in the gap hid the next attack from the onset check on 5 of 7 libraries with `009` placed by ear. The level is still set from the attack on. Where the notes end as written is summed in seconds (`n.start × spb + n.dur × spb`), not as `(n.start + n.dur) × spb`, because the port sums it that way: changing it breaks the bit-for-bit match with the web renderer. A Trim's lead-in is found between its handles, so Songs never play what's before the start handle; a Trim without its handles has none. `render()` places notes in start order and returns its events in that order; `export_golden.py` pairs them with the Song's notes by position, so it refuses a Song whose notes don't start in strictly increasing order (#287 review).
- **The Recording Pitch** is the energy-weighted median of the first burst's contour over its first 450 ms (`PITCH_WINDOW_S`), with a confidence of their share of the window (of the whole first burst when that is shorter) × (1 − spread / 3). Only when that finds no pitch, the same estimate runs once more over the 450 ms from the contour's first kept frame (#282, ADR 0017), so a tone that comes after the head counts; it never runs for a fart the head finds a pitch in. Its coverage is counted as the head counts it, never against the frames left after that frame, or a few quiet frames at the end of a short first burst would be full coverage: the owner's fart `107` got a false note at 45 Hz, pYIN's floor, that way (#295).
- **A Trim** (`trim_bounds()`, ADR 0019) cuts the handles at whole samples (round(seconds × SR)), then finds the fart between them as `note_segment()` finds a Recording's, so a Trim always lies within its handles and its onset lands on the beat as an untrimmed fart's does. Any Recording can be trimmed (#314). Nothing re-reads a trimmed Recording's pitch: a Melodic one keeps its Recording Pitch, shortest note and settle time, and a placed one stays on its note. The web's `trimBounds()`, `trimOf()` and `renderSource()` give the same.
- **Pitch confidence** passes at 0.2, loose on purpose: the audition is the real gate (#208 raised it from 0.1).
- **The settle cost** (20 per note played wholly before its Recording settles, #92) was picked from 15, 20, 30, 40, 80 and 160, and per-second forms, by renders passing; 40 and above make a full-library Song worse.
- **The silence cost** (30 per second a Recording leaves silent, #205) was picked from 0, 10, 20, 30, 40, 60 and 80 on the 12 app Songs with the Fixture Library. Every value keeps all 12 passing with no Octave Moves, and 30 is the smallest that plays Happy Birthday's last half note in full (0.41 to 1.18 s). Above 30, sounding time gains little (90.0% to at most 93.2%) and single Songs lose on-pitch time.
- **A Placed Recording** (`placed()`, ADR 0015) is a Melodic Recording at the note the user chose by ear, at its own length. Any Recording can be placed: a Noise-like one on a note, or a Melodic one on another note than analysis found (#314). The placement outlasts a later analysis that finds a Recording Pitch, and stays the one used: the user chose it (ADR 0017). Nothing measured its pitch, so its shortest note (`PLACED_MIN_NOTE_S`, 0.6 s) is the longest length `audition()` tries, the slowest a Melodic Recording reads back on, and it counts as settled from its start. `render()` plays it raw, like any fart: the note says where it goes, never how it sounds. The web's `placedAutoMapRecording()` makes the same.
- **`NOTE_FILL`** (1.0) and its sweep are in [ADR 0011](../../docs/adr/0011-unlocking-needs-farts-as-long-as-notes.md); the planner's moves, ranking and costs in [The reference planner](../../fixtures/golden/planner.md).
- **A note shorter than the gap** (`GAP_S`, 25 ms) has no time left to sound, not a negative time (#332). A negative slot made `unsettled_cost()` about −1e8 a note, since it divides by `max(1e-9, slot)`, so the solver's penalties no longer ranked choices: Bolero's ornaments crashed `smallest_ids()`, or left a pitch uncovered that a Recording covers. The solver's penalties stay above any choice's cost only while costs are never negative. `smallest_ids()` raises if no choice for a pitch is optimal, since the last `solve()` was optimal: without one, the next pitch's Recording would land on this pitch. `apps/web/src/lib/engine/auto-map.test.ts` checks the same on the web.
- **Bar lines** (`melody.bar_lines`): a bar is a pickup, not a bar, when it's shorter than the bar after it and either opens the score or completes a bar split at a repeat sign (3/4 and 1/4 before 4/4). PDMX's MIDI writes a pickup as its own short bar, and each half of a split bar as its own time signature, so the bar before the second half must be a whole time-signature segment: two whole 2/4 bars before 4/4 stay two bars.
- **The importer** takes the track with the highest mean pitch, leaving out a track whose every note is on the drum channel (MIDI channel 10) or an unpitched General MIDI percussive program: Agogo, Woodblock, Taiko Drum, Synth Drum and Reverse Cymbal (0-based programs 113, 115, 116, 118, 119). Tinkle Bell, Steel Drums and Melodic Tom can carry a tune. A score of only percussion still imports. It keeps the highest note at each onset only when that note begins there: an inner voice starting under a held top note is dropped and counted, not allowed to chop the melody note. Engraving software exports notes slightly short of their written value, so a note's slot runs to the next onset unless a real rest follows: a gap of a full beat, or longer than the note itself.
